"Rent income" or "Rental income"

My understanding is that "rental" is a word implying that income is being received from a property one owns and leases to a tenant. "Rent" is used more in the sense of what a tenant pays a landlord.

I am looking specifically for a two or three word phrase to be used in a list of possible sources of income.

Which option is more correct?

"rent income"

or

"rental income"

My first reaction is to say that the correct term is

Rental income

I believe that this is the term used on UK government tax forms (but do not have one available to check).

This is also confirmed by the following dictionary definitions (note the ones in bold):

rental1 noun
1. the act of renting.
2. money paid as rent.
3. income derived from money paid as rent.
4. a list of rent owed to a property owner by tenants; a rent-roll.
5. property available for renting.

rental2 noun
an amount paid or received as rent
